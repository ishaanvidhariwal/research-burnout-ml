03  —  The Data

Where the evidence came from

321 observations, four continuous predictors, one constructed target. The raw responses stay private; everything needed to understand and check the analysis is here.

🔒
Raw participant data is not distributed. Responses are from students and include wellbeing items, so the individual-level file is kept private. Published instead: the data dictionary below, aggregate statistics, the correlation structure, a synthetic sample with identical columns, and the full analysis code.

3.3Scale reliability
3.4Descriptive statistics

All five composites are on a 1–5 metric. Means cluster near the scale midpoint, between 3.31 and 3.67 — moderate-to-high academic stress, psychological distress and burnout, alongside moderate self-efficacy and social support. Distributions are approximately symmetric (all |skew| < .50) and mildly platykurtic, which supports the use of parametric tests. The burnout composite has a median of 3.29, the value used for the median split. Full per-scale means and standard deviations are in Table 2 of the manuscript.

3.5Exploratory relationships
Collinearity to keep in mind Academic stress and psychological distress correlate at r = .683. They share a large amount of variance, which is why academic stress loses its unique predictive contribution once distress is in the model (Results §6.4).

What I noticed. Psychological distress has the strongest apparent link to burnout, and the two protective factors behave as theory predicts. But none of this is causal: the design is cross-sectional, so it is equally consistent with burnout producing distress, reduced help-seeking, and lower efficacy through feedback.

3.6Missing data & synthetic sample

Item-level missingness was under 1%. Complete-case analysis was applied per scale; the impact on power was negligible. Duplicate submissions were removed through response-pattern analysis before the 321-row dataset was finalised.

A synthetic sample — same columns, same value ranges, no real responses — is provided in the repository at data/synthetic_sample.csv so the pipeline can be run end to end without the private data. It reproduces the column structure, not the real distributions or effects.
