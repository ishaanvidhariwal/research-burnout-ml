6.2Descriptive statistics

All composites sit on a 1–5 metric with means between 3.31 and 3.67, approximately symmetric (all |skew| < .50) and mildly platykurtic — adequate for parametric analysis. The burnout composite median is 3.29. Per-scale means and SDs are in Table 2 of the manuscript.

6.4Multiple linear regression

Predicting the continuous burnout score from all four factors at once: F(4, 316) = 110.41, p < .001, R² = .583 (adjusted R² = .578).

The four factors together account for 58.3% of the variance in burnout scores.

The remaining ~42% is other factors, measurement error, and the multidimensionality of the burnout composite.
