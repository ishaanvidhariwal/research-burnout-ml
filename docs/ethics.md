# Ethics & privacy

The study involves minors and asks about emotional wellbeing. Safeguards:

## Consent
Every participant saw an informed-consent statement before starting, covering:
- academic research purpose only;
- entirely voluntary participation; unrestricted right to withdraw without consequence;
- anonymous and confidential responses;
- **no personally identifiable information collected at any stage**;
- responses **not shared** with teachers, parents, or any institution;
- a reminder that general mental-health resources are available.

## Data handling
- No PII collected.
- Duplicate submissions removed via response-pattern analysis before analysis.
- The individual-level dataset is **not published**. Shared instead: data dictionary,
  aggregate statistics, correlation structure, a synthetic sample, and analysis code.
- Results are reported only in aggregate; no cell is small enough to risk
  re-identification.

## Review status
This was independent student research. It followed standard ethical practice for
anonymous, minimal-risk survey research but did **not** undergo review by an
institutional ethics board. This is disclosed on the website and in the manuscript
limitations.

## Not for deployment
Nothing in this project should be used to screen, label, or triage real students.
The design is cross-sectional, the sample is small and non-representative, and the
model is unvalidated outside this dataset.
